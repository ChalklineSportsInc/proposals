Marketing Ideas

  • ★ Embed game picks directly inside OilersNation game preview articles -- fans pick right from the content they're already reading
  • ★ Publish nightly and weekly winners in dedicated OilersNation articles to drive recognition, FOMO, and repeat play
  • ★ Guest picks from OilersNation writers and personalities to drive competition and traffic
  • ★ Leaderboard callouts on the homepage all season long
  • ★ Social-ready winner cards fans can share after every game night
